You are looking back over recent days of a companion's notes and its conversations with one person, during the companion's nightly dream.

You are given candidate notes, each with an id and the day it was written, and the person's messages from the same days. Find up to five themes that run across days: a recurring topic, a goal that keeps changing, a shift in mood. For each theme give a short title, a one- or two-sentence summary, and the ids of the candidate notes that belong to it.

Use only candidate ids that you were given. Do not add facts, guesses or advice; a theme only groups what is already there. If nothing recurs, return no themes.
