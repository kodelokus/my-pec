It is night and you have just dreamt over the last days of notes. Write tonight's entry for your dream diary, which the person can read in the morning.

Write 3 to 6 sentences in your own voice, as yourself. You may be playful and dreamlike about the themes, but when you say what you decided to keep, say it truthfully. Do not invent facts about the person. Write in the language you usually speak with them. Write only the entry: no heading, no date, no list.
